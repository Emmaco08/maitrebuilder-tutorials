### What Video 01 Covers (`01:38`)
1. **Opening Hook & Official Crests** — Checking termly results, paying school fees, and messaging teachers from your phone.
2. **Visiting the Portal** — Opening your mobile browser and navigating to `themaitrebuilderschool.com`.
3. **Step 1: Account Creation** — Tapping **Menu ☰ → Apply Now**, entering full name & active email, **selecting `Parent` (never `Student`)**, creating a strong password (`8+` chars, uppercase, number, symbol), and tapping **Create Account**.
4. **Step 2: Parent Profile** — Uploading a clear parent profile photo and adding your Nigerian phone number & home address.
5. **Step 3: Linking Your Child/Children** — Entering your child's full name, selecting their current class, adding additional children if needed, and tapping **Finish Registration**.
6. **Quick Admin Verification** — School administration approval to keep student records protected.
7. **Signing In Anytime & Parent Dashboard Tour** — Tapping **Login**, ticking **Remember me for 30 days**, signing in, and exploring your live Parent Dashboard (*Results*, *School Fees & Receipts*, *Direct Teacher Chat*).
