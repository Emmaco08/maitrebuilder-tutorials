### What Video 01 Covers
1. **Opening Hook & Official Crests** — Checking termly results, paying school fees, and messaging teachers right from your phone.
2. **Visiting the Portal** — Opening your phone browser and visiting `themaitrebuilderschool.com`.
3. **Step 1: Account Creation** — Tapping **Menu ☰ → Apply Now**, entering your full name & active email, **always tapping `Parent` (never `Student`)**, creating a strong password (`8+` chars, uppercase, number, symbol), and tapping **Create Account**.
4. **Step 2: Parent Profile** — Uploading a clear profile photo and entering your Nigerian phone number & home address.
5. **Step 3: Adding Your Child/Children** — Entering your child's full name, selecting their current class, adding additional children if enrolled, and tapping **Finish Registration**.
6. **Quick Admin Verification** — School administration verification to protect student records.
7. **Signing In & Parent Dashboard** — Tapping **Login**, ticking **Remember me for 30 days**, signing in, and touring your Parent Dashboard (*Results*, *Fees & Receipts*, *Teacher Chat*).
